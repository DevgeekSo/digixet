Mintwave drinks

Performance-focused nutrition brand helping active individuals improve energy and daily consistency.

Category

Food & Beverage

Platforms
Timeline

50 Days

Key focus

Fitness narratives

The Challenge

The brand was consistently publishing content and running ads, but results were unpredictable. some creatives performed well initially but dropped in performance as soon as budgets increased.

Content lacked clear differentiation and felt repetitive within a crowded market. while there was steady effort and investment, there was no structured system to maintain and scale performance.

Content lacked clear differentiation and felt repetitive within a crowded market. While there was steady effort and investment, there was no structured system to maintain and scale performance.

Our Strategy

We refined the brand’s positioning and focused on building a content system designed for repeatable performance.

Each reel was structured around clear problem-to-solution storytelling, with strong hooks tailored for attention and retention. we tested multiple variations of messaging, visuals, and formats to identify what resonated most.

Instead of relying on one-off viral content, we created scalable formats that could be optimized over time — turning short-form content into a predictable growth channel.

No items found.

The Result

90 days. Performance backed by real data.

We partnered with 15 lifestyle and fitness creators across TikTok and Instagram who aligned with Mintwave’s refreshing, health-first positioning. Each creator showcased the product in everyday routines—making it feel natural, not promotional.
